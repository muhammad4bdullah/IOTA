OPERATING NOTES

Fileditch links + free downloads

  1. Fileditch hosts the installer files; Cloudflare Pages and D1 host the IOTA site, catalog, and download-request counts. No R2 bucket is needed.
  2. Fileditch says files with no access for 45 days may become eligible for deletion when storage needs reclaiming. Check its current terms and keep backups.
  3. Fileditch may show its own ads on regular file pages. IOTA ads are separate and should only be enabled after your ad provider approves the site and required privacy/consent setup is complete.
